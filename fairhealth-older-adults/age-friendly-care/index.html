4Ms of Age-Friendly Care

The 4Ms framework was created by the Institute for Healthcare Improvement and The John A. Hartford Foundation to help older adults get coordinated, higher-quality care.

The 4Ms include:

What Matters

Align your care with your goals, values and the kind of care or treatment you want to have. Share what is important to you with your healthcare provider and family caregiver.

Medication

Talk to your healthcare provider and caregiver about the prescription drugs you are taking and any side effects that affect your Mobility or your Mind (Mentation).

Mind (Mentation)

You are more at risk for mental health conditions like dementia, depression and delirium as you age. Share any changes in your memory or mood with your healthcare provider and caregiver. That way, you can treat any issues early.

Mobility

Work with your healthcare provider to create an exercise plan to help keep you flexible and mobile. It can also help you get around safely and stay active as you age.
