Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias

Alzheimer's disease is a brain disorder that affects memory, thinking and behavior over time. It is the most common form of dementia. Related conditions include vascular dementia, Lewy body dementia and frontotemporal dementia. Receiving a diagnosis may be overwhelming for you and your caregiver. You and your caregiver will need clear information to decide on care that meets your needs.

Use FAIR Health's free tools and resources to help you and your caregiver plan for Alzheimer's disease care. These resources were created to support shared decision making with cost information among you, your caregiver and your healthcare providers.

These tools and resources were developed with funding from The John A. Hartford Foundation.

The tools, also known as decision aids, are not meant to be medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. They are meant to share information to help you engage in shared decision making with your healthcare providers. Each patient's condition will vary. The clinical options in the decision aids should be discussed with your healthcare provider.
